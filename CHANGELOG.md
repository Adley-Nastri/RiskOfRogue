# Changelog

## Unreleased (testing)

- Optional Risk of Rain 2 music in runs, in place of Deadzone's: one calm stage song per run and a
  different teleporter track every wave, switched at Deadzone's own music cues with timings measured
  from the vanilla game. Follows the Music and Master sliders; silent outside runs. Turn it off in
  `music.txt`. Songs are extracted from your own RoR2 install (extractor still to come). See docs/MUSIC.md.

## 1.0.0 (first public release)

- 74 Risk of Rain 2 items (Common, Uncommon, Legendary, base game and DLCs) as native Deadzone: Rogue items, offered in item orbs.
- Deadzone's elements and keywords: elemental damage and statuses, Critical Hits, Mark.
- Descriptions in Deadzone's own style with element and keyword icons; RoR2 flavour text kept.
- Deadzone rarity borders in the inventory and on item-orb cards.
- RoR2 buff icons above the health bar and short proc messages.
- RoR2 difficulty clock that matches Deadzone's run timer to the second; enemies that spawn later in a run are tougher.
- The mod's HUD hides with Deadzone's HUD and freezes while the game is paused (solo).
- RoR2 items show in the end-of-run screen's Items row.
- The main and pause menus show the Risk of Rogue version next to Deadzone's.
- Icon extractor that takes the art from your own Risk of Rain 2 install; no RoR2 assets are shipped.
